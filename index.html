THE CHALLENGE

Training should not end with a certificate.

Traditional skilling systems often track attendance, assessments and certificates — but fail to track what happens after training.

📋

Limited Tracking

Employment outcomes are often difficult to track after a trainee completes a program.

🔗

Disconnected Data

Different programs may use different identifiers, making data comparison difficult.

🧩

Hidden Skill Gaps

Training programs may not always match the skills currently demanded by employers.

OUR SOLUTION

From training data to real-world impact.

SkillTrack connects the complete journey of a trainee and converts data into actionable insights.
